About Oracle Red Bull Racing

Oracle Red Bull Racing is a Formula 1 team based in Milton Keynes, United Kingdom. The team began racing under the Red Bull name in 2005 and developed into one of the sport’s most successful constructors.

From Sebastian Vettel’s four-title run to Max Verstappen’s championship years, the team’s story has been shaped by bold car design, fast race execution and a constant search for performance. The 2026 RB22 begins a new chapter with a Red Bull Ford power unit.

Achievements

  • 6 Constructors’ Championships: 2010–2013 and 2022–2023
  • 8 Drivers’ Championships with Vettel and Verstappen
  • First Grand Prix victory: China, 2009
  • The RB19 delivered a record-setting 2023 season

Home Base

Instead of a stadium, a Formula 1 team has a factory and technology campus. Red Bull Racing designs, builds and prepares its cars in Milton Keynes before taking them to circuits around the world.

  • Red Bull Technology Campus
  • Milton Keynes, United Kingdom

Racing Approach

Aerodynamics, engineering, strategy and pit crew precision all matter in Formula 1. The team’s 2026 car and power unit are developed together at its Milton Keynes campus.

On-Track Battles

Every Grand Prix is a contest of pace and decisions. Red Bull Racing’s championship campaigns have included close battles against teams such as Ferrari, Mercedes and McLaren.
